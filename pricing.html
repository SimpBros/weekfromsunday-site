Pricing questions

Do you change my pastor's words?

No. Clips use your pastor's own voice and words, cut for length and captioned. Captions are checked word for word against the audio. Anything we write ourselves (emails, guides, posts) is a draft in plain language for your approval, and we never put words in your pastor's mouth. Direct quotes are marked and match the sermon.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for transcription and first drafts, and we say so plainly. Every pack is read by a person before it reaches you, and nothing is posted until your pastor or staff approves it. We never publish Scripture we haven't checked word for word against a public-domain text.

Which Bible translation do you quote?

We quote public-domain translations by default: the Berean Standard Bible (BSB) or WEB in English, and Reina-Valera 1909 in Spanish. If your church licenses another translation, tell us at intake and we'll use your text exactly as you provide it. Your pastor's spoken quotations in clips are left as spoken.

What do we have to do each week?

Send us the sermon (a YouTube or Facebook link, or an audio/video file) by Monday 10 AM your time. We deliver your pack by Wednesday 10 AM. Approving it takes about five minutes.

Do we need to give you passwords?

No. Approved files are delivered in a shared folder you control. For Google posts and review replies, you can add us as a manager on your Google Business Profile, which you can remove anytime, or you can paste the approved text yourself.

Who owns the content?

You do. Everything we make from your sermons belongs to your church. We don't reuse your sermons or your people's images in our own marketing without written permission.

Is there a contract?

No. It's month to month. Cancel before your next billing date and you won't be charged again. Annual prepay saves 10% if you'd like it.

What does the free sample include?

Reply yes and we'll turn one recent public sermon into a sample: 2 captioned clips, 2 graphics and a draft devotional email, in about three business days. There is no card, no obligation, and we never post anything for you.

What's the 14-day pilot?

If you like the sample, we run the full Complete service for 14 days (two weekly packs) at no charge and no card. On day 12 we ask if you'd like to continue. If not, you owe nothing.

We're a bilingual church. How does Spanish work?

The Bilingual plan produces every piece in both languages. Spanish text is drafted by our team and checked by a native speaker; your pastor or a trusted member can have the final word. Clips keep your pastor's original audio. For an English sermon we add Spanish captions (and the reverse for a Spanish sermon), so each clip can reach both congregations.

What if our sermon runs long or we have a guest speaker?

Sermons up to 60 minutes are included. For guest speakers, we ask for the speaker's permission to cut clips, which you can confirm in the intake or per sermon.
