The words

Devotional email

Subject: Your week in Psalm 23: five quiet minutes

Day 3: He is with me

"Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for You are with me." Psalm 23:4 (BSB)

Name one hard thing today and say out loud: "You are with me." Then write one line about what you noticed.

Small-group guide (excerpt)

Warm-up. When have you been completely lost? What helped you find your way?

Discuss. The psalm is mostly about what God does, not what we do. Where do you try to do God's part?

This week. For five days, make God your first call with a joy and your first call with a worry.
