Terms & privacy

Plain-language terms.

Draft for attorney review. Written in plain English for pastors. Have a lawyer review before launch.

The service

Each week we turn one sermon (up to 60 minutes) into the pack described on your plan. We deliver it for your approval within 48 hours of receiving the sermon by the Monday 10 AM deadline.

Approval

Nothing is published, scheduled or sent on your behalf until you approve it. Where you give us manager access to your Google Business Profile, we use it only for items you approved.

Ownership

You own the content we create for you. You give us permission to process your sermon files to make it. We don't publish your sermons, clips, images or name in our own marketing without written permission.

AI

We use AI tools for transcription and first drafts. A person reviews every pack. We don't train AI models on your sermons and we don't sell or share your data.

Scripture

We quote public-domain translations by default (BSB, WEB, Reina-Valera 1909). If you ask us to use a licensed translation, you confirm that you hold the right to use it.

Third-party material

We don't add music or video that we don't have the right to use. If your sermon includes third-party clips or music, tell us, and we'll leave those moments out of anything we cut.

Billing & cancelling

Plans are billed monthly in advance. Cancel any time before your next billing date and you won't be charged again. If your first paid month wasn't useful, tell us within 30 days and we'll refund it. Annual prepay (10% off) is refundable pro rata in the first 30 days.

Pilot & sample

The free sample and 14-day pilot cost nothing and need no card. You owe nothing if you stop.

No promises about growth

We prepare communication tools. We can't promise attendance, giving or engagement, and we won't say we can.

Your data

We collect what's on the intake form and the sermon files you send. We store them only to serve you, delete sermon files within 90 days after you cancel (sooner on request), and never sell data. Questions: hello@weekfromsunday.com.
